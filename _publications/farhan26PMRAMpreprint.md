title: "Benchmarking CNN and Vision Transformer Architectures for Four-Class Brain Tumor Classification on the PMRAM Bangladeshi MRI Dataset"
description: "Accurate brain tumor diagnosis depends on early detection, yet most publicly available magnetic resonance imaging (MRI) datasets and the deep learning models benchmarked on them are drawn from Western populations, leaving South Asian clinical settings comparatively underserved. This paper reports a systematic benchmarking study on Publicly Available MRI Dataset for Radiological Analysis and Modeling (PMRAM), a Bangladeshi brain MRI dataset, for the four-class problem of separating glioma, meningioma, pituitary tumor and tumor-free scans. We fine-tuned seven pretrained convolutional neural networks (CNN) (ResNet18, ResNet50, VGG16, VGG19, EfficientNet-B1, MobileNetV2, and DenseNet121), each under three optimizers (AdamW, Adam, and SGD) with a cosine-annealed learning rate, giving 21 CNN configurations. To probe whether global self-attention adds value on this data, we evaluated four Vision Transformer (ViT) variants (ViT-Base, ViT-Small, DeiT-Base and BEiT-Base) under the same optimizer protocol, adding 12 further configurations. The five strongest configurations were then re-assessed with Stratified 5-Fold Cross-Validation to confirm that their accuracy was not an artifact of a single fortunate split. ResNet50 with AdamW gave the best cross-validated result, 98.74% mean accuracy, narrowly ahead of the leading transformer. Taken together, the experiments show that transfer learning, paired with disciplined validation, already reaches clinically encouraging accuracy on a modest regional dataset, and they set the stage for our planned work on explainable AI (XAI) and hybrid CNN–Transformer models."
