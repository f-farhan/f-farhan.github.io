I am currently working as a Lecturer in the [Department of Electrical and Electronic Engineering](https://www.aust.edu/eee) at [Ahsanullah University of Science and Technology (AUST)](https://www.aust.edu/), Dhaka, Bangladesh. My research interests include biomedical optics, medical imaging, signal processing, and machine learning and deep learning for healthcare. I am particularly interested in developing methods for non-contact physiological measurement and analysing biomedical images and signals to support healthcare research.

Between 2023 and 2025, I pursued the [Erasmus Mundus Joint Master’s Degree in Medical Imaging and Applications (MAIA)](https://maiamaster.udg.edu/) with an Erasmus Mundus scholarship. The programme was co-ordinated by three European universities: the [University of Girona](https://www.udg.edu/) in Spain, [Université Bourgogne Europe](https://www.ube.fr/) in France, and the [University of Cassino and Southern Lazio](https://www.unicas.it/) in Italy. Studying across different academic and cultural environments strengthened my interest in interdisciplinary research connecting engineering, computing, and medical diagnosis.

In 2025, I worked as a Graduate Research Intern at the [Laboratoire Imagerie et Vision Artificielle (ImViA)](https://imvia.ube.fr/) in Dijon, France, under the supervision of [Prof. Yannick Benezeth](https://imvia.ube.fr/membres/benezeth-yannick/). My master’s thesis, “Design and Validation of Multimodal and Lightweight Handheld ST-rPPG Acquisition Systems for Physiological Measurements,” explored camera-based approaches to measuring physiological information. I investigated heart-rate estimation and the spatial distribution of microvascular perfusion from wrist videos, examining how polarization, spectral filtering, and motion compensation influence measurement quality. This research led to our [publication on vascular perfusion mapping and heart-rate estimation](https://doi.org/10.1088/2057-1976/ae1e82) in *Biomedical Physics & Engineering Express*.

Before my master’s studies, I completed my B.Sc. in Electrical and Electronic Engineering at AUST in 2021, graduating first in my class. I received the Khan Bahadur Ahsanullah Padak, in 2025 for my undergraduate academic achievement. My undergraduate thesis focused on pattern recognition through the development of a Raspberry Pi-based speaker recognition system, providing an early foundation in signal processing, machine learning, and embedded implementation.

I first joined AUST as a Lecturer in 2021 and taught until 2023 before pursuing my master’s studies in Europe. I returned to AUST in August 2025 as a Lecturer. Earlier in my academic career, I also worked as a Teaching Assistant at [East Delta University](https://www.eastdelta.edu.bd/). Alongside teaching, my research has included EEG analysis, computer-aided medical diagnosis, and image classification, with publications in reputed journals. My current work continues to explore how learning-based methods and imaging technologies can address practical biomedical problems.

I enjoy making technical ideas easier to understand through practical examples, programming exercises, and interactive learning tools. I develop educational web applications and laboratory activities that help students connect mathematical concepts with observable results. Beyond teaching and research, I served as Treasurer of the AUST Career Development Club in 2022, 2026 and previously served as Vice President of the AUST EEE Society in 2020. Outside academia, I enjoy travelling, photography, graphic design, basketball, and table tennis. I welcome opportunities to collaborate on biomedical imaging, physiological sensing, and AI for healthcare.
